KEY DETAILS:

  • Rate::from_khz(400)
    └─ This is the ESP HAL's way to specify I2C frequency
    └─ 400 = kilohertz
    └─ 400 kHz = 2.5 microsecond clock period (4x faster than 100 kHz)

  • I2C is fast enough to complete before USB interrupts interfere
  • SSD1306 OLED supports 400 kHz (I2C Fast Mode)
  • ESP32-S3 supports up to 1000 kHz
